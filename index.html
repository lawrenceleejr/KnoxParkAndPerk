A road-safety program for downtown Knoxville

Ride from last call to first cup.

Drove downtown, then decided not to drive home? Good call. Show your bartender your booked rideshare or an activated transit ticket, and get a card good for a free large coffee at a downtown shop — for the morning you come back for your car. Consider it a thanks for keeping the roads safe.

For patrons

Four steps. Two brews. Safer roads.

You don’t have to choose between leaving your car and getting stranded downtown. Pick-Me-Up turns the responsible choice into the rewarding one.

1

Park

Park in a municipal garage or lot downtown.

2

Catch a ride

At the end of the night, book an Uber, Lyft, or cab — or hop a KAT bus — home instead of driving. Leave the car; downtown’s public garages and lots are free after 6 pm and all weekend.

3

Show it & grab your card

Show your rideshare screen or activated transit ticket to bar staff before you head out. They stamp and hand over a Morning Pick-Me-Up card — one per ride. (Sharing a Lyft? The booker gets the card.)

4

Coffee & car

Ride KAT back downtown free on the card, claim your free large coffee within a day, grab your car, and drive home — sober and caffeinated.

Why it matters

Safer roads start with an easy reason to leave the car.

Impaired driving hurts real people on Knoxville’s roads every year. The single biggest reason someone drives home after drinking downtown is the car itself — nobody wants the hassle of coming back for it. Pick-Me-Up removes that reason: leave the car, get home safe, and let your car be the reason you’re back downtown in the morning.

Questions

Frequently asked

What counts as “showing my ride”?

A confirmed or just-booked rideshare (Uber/Lyft), a taxi receipt, an activated Knoxville Area Transit (KAT) bus ticket, or a designated-driver pickup at the bartender’s discretion. The point is simple: you’re not driving.

What about my car overnight?

Good news: downtown’s public parking — garages and surface lots — is free after 6 pm on weeknights and all weekend, so leaving your car overnight won’t cost you a thing. They’re all on the map. Just grab the car in the morning when you come back for your coffee. (A couple of sites price special events or Saturday daytime differently, so give the posted signage a glance.)

Can I get more than one card?

It’s one card per booked ride — the ride is the ticket. Cards are date-stamped, bar-marked, and serialized, and are valid for one day from issue. If your whole group piles into one Uber, that’s one card (whoever booked it gets the coffee — choose your friends wisely).

What about designated drivers?

If someone at the table is driving everyone else home and hasn’t had a drop to drink, a bartender can hand them a card as a thanks too — that’s entirely their call. Being the sober ride home is exactly the behavior this program exists to reward.

The card gets me free transit too?

Yes — we call it “Hair of the KAT.” While your Pick-Me-Up card is valid, show it to board any Knoxville Area Transit (KAT) bus and ride free, including your morning trip back downtown to pick up your car. One card, coffee and the ride to go get it.

What if I didn’t drive downtown?

The program is designed for people whose car is parked downtown — that’s the moment the risky decision happens. But bartenders have discretion; the goal is safe rides home, not paperwork.

Who pays for the coffee?

The funding model is being designed right now with our partners, drawing on sponsorships and partner venues. What’s already fixed: the perk is always free for the person who took the safe ride home.

How can I support this?

Tip a little extra at the bar and the coffee shop — easy to do when a ride you skipped just saved you the cost of one. Use the card as an excuse to try a partner business you haven’t been to yet; the roster’s on the map. And once you’ve found a favorite, keep coming back — repeat customers are exactly what makes the math work for the bars and shops carrying this.

Does the map track me?

No. The map is OpenStreetMap data (drawn by CARTO), and it only loads if you scroll down to it. If you tap “find the coffee near me,” your browser asks your permission first, and your location is used right there on your phone to work out which shop is closest — it isn’t sent to us or stored anywhere. Same as the cards themselves: this program collects nothing about you.

Is this a City of Knoxville program?

Knox Pick-Me-Up is a proposed public-private partnership, currently in the pilot design phase, seeking partnership with the City of Knoxville, the Knoxville Police Department, and downtown businesses.
